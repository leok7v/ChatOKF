---
type: Recipe
title: Sunday roast chicken
description: Dry-brined overnight, roasted hot on a bed of vegetables,
  and the thermometer is the only part that matters.
tags: [kitchen, chicken, sunday, dinner]
---

Salt the bird all over the day before and leave it uncovered in the fridge.
Dry skin is crisp skin, and the salt gets all the way in.

425F on top of halved potatoes and onions,
[rosemary](/garden/rosemary.md) and lemon in the cavity. About an hour for
a four-pound bird.

165F in the thickest part of the thigh, not the breast. Everything else is
guessing. Rest 15 minutes.

Carcass goes into stock the same night.
