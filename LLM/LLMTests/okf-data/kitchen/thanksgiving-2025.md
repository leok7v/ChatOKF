Fourteen people. Spatchcocked the turkey, which cooked in 90 minutes and
freed the oven, and that part was right.

Dry anyway, because I pulled it at 175F in the breast out of nerves. 157F
and a rest is the number.

Do again: spatchcock, make the stock two days ahead, gravy the morning of.
Change: brine wet not dry, two pies not three, and start the
[rolls](/kitchen/focaccia.md) dough Wednesday night.
