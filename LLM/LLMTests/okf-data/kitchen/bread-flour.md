---
type: Reference
title: Bread flour and why the protein number matters
description: Bread flour is around 12 to 13 percent protein against 10
  to 11 for all-purpose, which is what holds a high-hydration dough
  together.
tags: [kitchen, baking, reference, flour]
---

King Arthur bread flour, 12.7 percent protein. All-purpose is nearer 11.7,
and supermarket store brands can be 10.

More protein means more gluten, which means a dough at 75 percent hydration
holds its shape instead of spreading into a puddle.

Substituting all-purpose into
[the sourdough loaf](/kitchen/sourdough-loaf.md) works if you drop the
water by about 25 grams.

Whole wheat drinks more water and ferments faster. Above about 20 percent
of the total it changes the schedule.
