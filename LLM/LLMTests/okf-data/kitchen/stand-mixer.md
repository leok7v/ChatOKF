A KitchenAid Artisan, 5 quart, tilt head.

Earns its counter space for: enriched doughs like
[croissants](/kitchen/croissants.md), meringue and whipped cream, and
creaming butter for [cookies](/kitchen/chocolate-chip-cookies.md).

Does not earn it for: high-hydration bread, which does better with folds by
hand, or anything under two portions.

The tilt head flexes on stiff dough. The bowl-lift models do not, which is
what I would buy next time.
