---
type: Recipe
title: Rosemary focaccia in a sheet pan
description: A high-hydration sheet-pan focaccia with a long cold rise,
  dimpled under olive oil and rosemary.
tags: [kitchen, baking, bread, focaccia]
---

500g bread flour, 400g water, 10g salt, 7g instant yeast, plenty of olive
oil. Mix, fold a few times, cold rise in the fridge overnight or up to two
days.

Into an oiled half sheet pan, let it come to room temperature and relax for
two hours, then dimple it hard with oiled fingers all the way to the pan.
Olive oil on top, flaky salt,
[rosemary from the pot](/garden/rosemary.md).

450F for 25 minutes. The oil in the pan effectively fries the bottom, which
is the whole point.
