The dish from Hanoi: charred pork sitting in a warm bowl of nuoc cham
rather than on top of the noodles.

Sauce is fish sauce, sugar, rice vinegar, water, garlic and chile, mixed
until it tastes like all four things at once. Green papaya and carrot
pickled in it.

Pork belly slices and seasoned patties, grilled hard over charcoal.

Served with rice vermicelli and a plate of lettuce,
[mint](/garden/mint-containment.md), perilla and cilantro. You build each
bite yourself.

On the list for [the Vietnam leg](/travel/vietnam.md).
