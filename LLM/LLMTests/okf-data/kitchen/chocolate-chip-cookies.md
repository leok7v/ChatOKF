type: Recipe
title: Chocolate chip cookies with a 24-hour rest
description: Resting the dough a day before baking is the single change
  that made these better than the package recipe.
tags: [kitchen, baking, cookies]
---

Standard creamed-butter dough, but browned butter, a little more brown
sugar than white, chopped chocolate rather than chips, and then the dough
rests covered in the fridge for 24 to 36 hours.

The rest hydrates the flour and lets the sugars break down. The cookies
brown more evenly and taste like caramel instead of like sweet dough.

Flaky salt on top before baking. 350F, 11 minutes, pull them while the
centers still look underdone.
