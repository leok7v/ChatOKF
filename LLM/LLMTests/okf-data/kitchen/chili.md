---
type: Recipe
title: Weeknight chili with roasted poblanos
description: Ground beef, beans, and roasted poblanos from the garden,
  which is the part that makes it taste like more work than it was.
tags: [kitchen, dinner, weeknight, peppers]
---

Roast [poblanos](/garden/peppers.md) directly on the burner until black,
steam them in a covered bowl ten minutes, then the skins slip off.

Brown a pound of beef, onion, garlic, two tablespoons chili powder, one of
cumin, bloom the spices in the fat for a minute. Add the peppers, a can of
beans and a quart of
[frozen tomato sauce](/kitchen/tomato-sauce.md). Simmer 45 minutes.

A square of dark chocolate and a spoon of vinegar at the end. Sounds like a
stunt, is not.
