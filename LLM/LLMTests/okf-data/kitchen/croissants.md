type: Recipe
title: Croissants, once a year
description: Three days of laminating butter into dough, which is worth
  doing once and not worth doing often.
tags: [kitchen, baking, pastry, project]
---

Day one: dough, rest overnight. Day two: butter block, three letter folds
with an hour in the fridge between each. Day three: roll, cut, shape,
proof four to six hours, egg wash, bake at 400F.

The one thing that matters is temperature. Butter warmer than the dough
smears and you get bread. Colder and it shatters into shards and you get
layers that do not connect. Both happen in a warm kitchen in July.

Do this in February.
