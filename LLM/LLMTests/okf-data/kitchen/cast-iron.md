title: The cast iron skillet
description: Seasoning is polymerized oil, not stored grease, and the
  dishwasher is the only thing that truly ruins it.
tags: [kitchen, equipment, cast-iron]
---

A 12-inch Lodge, inherited, black and slick.

Seasoning is oil polymerized into a hard layer by heat. It is not a film of
grease sitting on the surface, which is why soap does not hurt it and why
wiping it with oil while cold does nothing.

Reseason: thin coat of neutral oil, wipe it back off so it looks dry, upside
down in a 450F oven for an hour.

It will do anything except acid for a long time and eggs. Tomato sauce for
twenty minutes strips it.
