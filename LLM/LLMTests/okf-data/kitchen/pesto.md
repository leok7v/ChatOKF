title: Basil pesto
description: Pesto in the food processor with the cheese added off the
  heat, and freezing it in an ice cube tray for winter.
tags: [kitchen, sauce, basil, summer]
---

Four packed cups [basil](/garden/basil.md), half cup pine nuts or walnuts,
two cloves garlic, three quarters cup olive oil, one cup parmesan, salt.

Nuts and garlic first, then basil in pulses, then oil in a stream, then the
cheese by hand at the end. Processing the cheese makes it pasty.

Blanching the basil ten seconds keeps it green for days instead of hours.
It costs a little flavor and it is worth it if you are freezing.

Freeze in an ice cube tray, oil on top, bag the cubes. Two cubes is dinner
for two in February.
