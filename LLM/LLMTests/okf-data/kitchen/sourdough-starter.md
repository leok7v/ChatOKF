Sourdough rises on wild yeast and lactobacilli rather than commercial
baker's yeast. The lactic acid is where the sour comes from, and it is also
why the bread keeps for days without going stale.

Mine started in 2021 from [bread flour](/kitchen/bread-flour.md) and tap
water left on the counter, and it took eleven days to get reliable.

Weekly routine: take it out of the fridge, discard all but a spoonful, feed
1:5:5 by weight starter to flour to water, leave it four to six hours until
it domes and smells like yogurt, back in the fridge.

Before baking, two feeds twelve hours apart on the counter. A starter
straight out of the fridge will not lift a
[loaf](/kitchen/sourdough-loaf.md).
