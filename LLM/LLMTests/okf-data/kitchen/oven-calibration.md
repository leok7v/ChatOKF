---
type: Note
title: The oven runs 25 degrees hot
description: An oven thermometer showed the dial reads low, which
  explains years of over-browned bottoms.
tags: [kitchen, equipment, oven, problem]
---

Hung a cheap oven thermometer from the middle rack. Set to 350F, it settles
at about 375F, and it overshoots to nearly 400F right after the preheat
beep.

So: everything comes down 25 degrees from the recipe, and I wait ten
minutes past the beep before anything goes in.

This explains years of dark bottoms on
[cookies](/kitchen/chocolate-chip-cookies.md) and a
[sourdough](/kitchen/sourdough-loaf.md) crust that set before the loaf
finished rising.

The built-in calibration offset only goes to plus or minus 35.
