---
type: Note
title: Scrambled eggs low and slow
description: Low heat, constant stirring, off the heat before they look
  done.
tags: [kitchen, eggs, breakfast, technique]
---

Low heat. Not medium-low, low. Butter, eggs beaten with a splash of cream,
stirred continuously with a rubber spatula for five or six minutes.

Off the heat while they still look slightly wet. Carryover finishes them
and there is no recovering from overcooked eggs.

Salt at the end. Salt at the beginning draws water out and makes them weep
on the plate.

[Cast iron](/kitchen/cast-iron.md) is the wrong pan for this. Nonstick.
