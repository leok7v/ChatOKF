---
type: Recipe
title: Banana bread for the black bananas
description: The standing recipe for bananas nobody ate, which tolerates
  almost any substitution.
tags: [kitchen, baking, quickbread, easy]
---

Three very black bananas, 1/3 cup melted butter, 3/4 cup sugar, one egg,
teaspoon vanilla, teaspoon baking soda, pinch salt, 1.5 cups flour. 350F
for an hour in a loaf pan.

Blacker is better. Yellow bananas make bland bread.

Tolerates: brown sugar for white, yogurt for some of the butter, grated
[zucchini](/garden/zucchini.md) folded in, walnuts, chocolate chips.

Does not tolerate overmixing. Stir until barely combined.
