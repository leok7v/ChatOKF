title: Caprese with the good tomatoes
description: Three ingredients, so it is entirely about the tomato, and
  it is only worth making in August.
tags: [kitchen, salad, summer, tomatoes]
---

Sliced tomato, fresh mozzarella, [basil](/garden/basil.md), olive oil,
flaky salt. That is the whole thing.

Only in August, only with [garden tomatoes](/garden/tomatoes.md) at room
temperature. A refrigerated tomato has no smell and the dish is nothing
without it.

Salt the tomato slices five minutes ahead and let them weep. Balsamic is
optional and, in the Italian version, wrong.
