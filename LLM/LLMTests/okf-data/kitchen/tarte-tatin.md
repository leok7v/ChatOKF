title: Tarte tatin
description: Upside-down caramelized apple tart, where the whole
  difficulty is taking the caramel far enough without panicking.
tags: [kitchen, baking, dessert, french]
---

Firm apples, halved, packed tight into a skillet over butter and sugar
cooked to a proper dark amber. Pastry over the top, tucked in, baked at
400F for half an hour, then inverted onto a plate.

Two failure modes: caramel pulled too early, which tastes like sweet apple
and nothing else, and apples too soft, which collapse into sauce.

Take the caramel darker than feels right. It is bitter in the pan and
correct on the plate.

Invert while hot. Wait and the sugar glues it down.
