---
type: Recipe
title: Pad thai that is not orange
description: Tamarind, fish sauce and palm sugar, cooked one portion at
  a time in a very hot pan.
tags: [kitchen, thai, noodles, weeknight]
---

The sauce is three things: tamarind paste, fish sauce, palm sugar, roughly
equal by volume, adjusted until it is sour first and sweet second. No
ketchup, which is where the orange comes from.

Soak the rice noodles in warm water, do not boil them. They finish in the
pan.

One portion at a time. Two portions in a home pan means steaming, and
steamed pad thai is porridge.

Dried shrimp, preserved radish, egg, tofu, garlic chives, peanuts, lime.
