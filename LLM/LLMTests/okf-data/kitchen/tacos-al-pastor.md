type: Recipe
title: Tacos al pastor without a trompo
description: Achiote and pineapple marinated pork, broiled on a sheet
  pan instead of a vertical spit.
tags: [kitchen, mexican, pork, tacos]
---

Pork shoulder sliced thin, marinated overnight in achiote paste, guajillo,
pineapple juice, vinegar, oregano and cumin.

No trompo, so: stack the slices on a skewer standing in a loaf pan, broil,
turn, and shave the outside off as it chars. Closer to the real thing than
laying the slices flat, which just steams them.

Corn tortillas, raw onion, [cilantro](/garden/cilantro-bolting.md),
pineapple, lime. Nothing else.
