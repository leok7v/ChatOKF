Attempted after [Oaxaca](/travel/oaxaca.md), from notes taken in a cooking
class there.

Chilhuacle negro, mulato, pasilla and guajillo chiles, toasted until they
smoke and then soaked. Sesame, almonds, raisins, plantain, tortilla burned
black on the comal, cinnamon, clove, chocolate. Everything toasted
separately, ground, then fried in lard before the stock goes in.

Mine came out bitter. Two reasons, I think: I burned the chiles rather than
toasting them, and I skipped the step of frying the paste long enough
before adding liquid.

Worth trying again. Not a weeknight.
