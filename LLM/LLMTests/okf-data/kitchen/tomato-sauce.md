---
type: Recipe
title: Freezer tomato sauce from the garden glut
description: What to do with twenty pounds of tomatoes in September: a
  long slow reduction, frozen flat in bags.
tags: [kitchen, sauce, tomatoes, preserving]
---

Halve [the tomatoes](/garden/tomatoes.md), no need to peel or seed. Onion,
garlic, olive oil, a long low simmer for two to three hours until it stops
looking watery. Stick blender at the end, or a food mill if you care about
skins.

Salt at the end, not the start, because the volume drops by half.

Freeze flat in quart bags. Twenty pounds gives about eight bags, which
lasts until March.

Goes on [pizza](/kitchen/pizza-dough.md) and into
[chili](/kitchen/chili.md).
