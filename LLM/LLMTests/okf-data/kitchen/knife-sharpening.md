type: Procedure
title: Sharpening the kitchen knives
description: A 1000/6000 waterstone twice a year and a honing rod
  weekly, which is most of what a home cook needs.
tags: [kitchen, equipment, knives, procedure]
---

Honing rod before most sessions. That straightens the edge, it does not
sharpen it, and it is why a knife feels dull after two weeks when the edge
is fine.

Twice a year on a 1000/6000 waterstone, soaked 15 minutes. About 15 degrees
per side for the Japanese knives, 20 for the German. Raise a burr along the
whole edge on the 1000, flip, match it, then refine on the 6000 and strop.

Test on paper, not on a thumb.
