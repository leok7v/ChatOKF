---
type: Recipe
title: Friday pizza dough
description: A 65 percent hydration dough balled and cold-fermented two
  days, stretched thin and baked as hot as the oven goes.
tags: [kitchen, baking, pizza, friday]
---

500g flour, 325g water, 10g salt, 3g instant yeast. Mix, rest 20 minutes,
knead briefly, divide into four balls, cold ferment 48 hours.

Out of the fridge two hours before. Stretch by hand, never a rolling pin,
which crushes the rim.

Steel on the top rack, oven at its maximum for 45 minutes first. Six to
seven minutes a pie.

Sauce is [the summer tomato sauce](/kitchen/tomato-sauce.md) uncooked and
thinned. Basil goes on after the bake, not before.
