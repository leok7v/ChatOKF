400g bread flour, 320g water, 8g salt, quarter teaspoon instant yeast.
Stir, cover, leave 12 to 18 hours. Fold twice, rest an hour, bake in a
preheated dutch oven at 450F, 30 minutes covered and 15 uncovered.

It genuinely works and it is nearly zero effort. The crumb is more open and
less flavorful than sourdough and it goes stale by the second day.

Superseded by [the sourdough loaf](/kitchen/sourdough-loaf.md). Keeping
this because it is still the right answer when I forget to feed the
starter.
