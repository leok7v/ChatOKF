---
type: Recipe
title: The weekend sourdough loaf
description: A 75 percent hydration white loaf with an overnight cold
  retard, which is the version that finally worked consistently.
tags: [kitchen, baking, bread, sourdough]
---

500g bread flour, 375g water, 100g active
[starter](/kitchen/sourdough-starter.md), 10g salt.

- Mix flour and water, rest one hour. Add starter and salt.
- Four sets of stretch and folds, 30 minutes apart.
- Bulk until risen about 50 percent, four to five hours at 72F.
- Shape, into a banneton, into the fridge overnight.
- Bake straight from the fridge at 500F in a preheated dutch oven, lid on
  20 minutes, lid off at 450F for 20 more.

The cold retard is what made this repeatable. It also builds flavor and
makes scoring possible.

This replaced [the no-knead loaf](/kitchen/no-knead-bread.md).
Oven runs hot, see [oven calibration](/kitchen/oven-calibration.md).
