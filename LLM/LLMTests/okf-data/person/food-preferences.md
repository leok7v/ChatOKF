Maya is vegetarian, eggs and dairy fine, and has been for two years. This
is not a phase and it does not get worked around with "just pick the meat
out".

Ben eats plain pasta, plain rice, cheese quesadillas, apples, and chicken
if it does not touch a sauce.

Joe and Dana eat anything, and the spicier the better for Joe.

So: most weeknight dinners are a base plus toppings that people assemble
themselves. [Tacos](/kitchen/tacos-al-pastor.md) work.
[Chili](/kitchen/chili.md) needs a meatless pot.
[Pad thai](/kitchen/pad-thai.md) is made twice, once with fish sauce and
once without, which is annoying and worth it.

Nobody in the house dislikes bread, which is convenient given
[the starter](/kitchen/sourdough-starter.md).
