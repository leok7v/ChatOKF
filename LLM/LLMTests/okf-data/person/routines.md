Weekday mornings are the swim run at 5:40. Joe is up anyway.

Tuesday and Wednesday in the office, so those are leftover nights.

Thursday is groceries. Friday is [pizza](/kitchen/pizza-dough.md), which
means the dough goes in the fridge Wednesday.

Saturday morning is the garden and whatever
[house project](/house/projects-2026.md) is current. Saturday night is
[family movie night](/watch/family-movie-night.md).

Sunday is [the sourdough bake](/kitchen/sourdough-loaf.md) and
[roast chicken](/kitchen/roast-chicken.md) often enough to be a pattern.

Nothing gets done between 6:30 and 8:30 on a weeknight. That is dinner and
homework.
