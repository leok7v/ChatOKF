Dana, 45, teaches middle school science. Off in the summer, which is when
travel is actually possible and also when the garden needs the most
attention.

Maya, 14, high school freshman, vegetarian since she was 12. Swim team,
practice five mornings a week at 6am.

Ben, 11, sixth grade. Eats about nine foods. Soccer in the fall.

A dog, Tilly, 60 pounds, who is the reason the vegetable beds are fenced
separately from [the deer fence](/garden/deer-fence.md).

Practical consequences: dinner is at 6:30 or it does not happen, travel is
June through early August or a school break, and half the meals have to
work without meat. See [food preferences](/person/food-preferences.md).
