title: Allergies and things to keep out of the kitchen
description: Dana is allergic to shellfish, seriously, and Ben gets
  hives from pistachios.
tags: [person, health, allergies, safety]
---

Dana: shellfish. Shrimp, crab, lobster. Genuine allergy, not a
sensitivity, and she carries an epinephrine autoinjector. Fish is fine.
Fish sauce is fine, which surprises people.

This rules out a lot of [pad thai](/kitchen/pad-thai.md) as normally
made, since dried shrimp is standard. Leave it out, add extra tamarind.

Ben: pistachios, hives. Other tree nuts are fine, including the walnuts in
[pesto](/kitchen/pesto.md).

Joe: nothing.

Cross-contamination matters for the shellfish and not for the pistachios.
