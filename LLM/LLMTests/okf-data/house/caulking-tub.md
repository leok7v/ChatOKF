---
type: Procedure
title: Recaulking the tub surround
description: Remove all the old caulk, dry it completely, fill the tub
  before you bead it.
tags: [house, bathroom, caulk, procedure]
---

Cut the old caulk out entirely with a razor and a caulk removal tool.
Caulking over old caulk is why it failed the first time. Wipe with alcohol,
let it dry a day.

Fill the tub with water before beading it. The tub deflects under load, and
a bead laid on an empty tub tears when someone stands in it.

100 percent silicone, not acrylic latex, for anything that stays wet. It is
harder to tool and it lasts five times as long.

Bead, then smooth with a wet finger in one pass. Stopping and starting
shows.
