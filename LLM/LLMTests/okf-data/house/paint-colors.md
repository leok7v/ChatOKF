Kept because "the beige one" is not a color.

- Living and dining: Benjamin Moore Revere Pewter HC-172, eggshell.
- Kitchen: Simply White OC-117, satin.
- Main bedroom: Sherwin-Williams Sea Salt SW 6204, eggshell.
- Bathrooms: Simply White, semi-gloss.
- Trim throughout: Simply White, semi-gloss.
- Ceilings: flat white, contractor grade, not worth specifying.

Leftover cans are labeled and in the basement on the metal shelf.

Touch-ups never quite match on a wall that has faded. Repaint the whole
wall corner to corner. See [drywall patch](/house/drywall-patch.md).
