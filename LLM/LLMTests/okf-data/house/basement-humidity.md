title: Basement humidity and the musty smell
description: Sixty-five percent relative humidity in summer, which is
  above the mold threshold, and the grading outside is part of it.
tags: [house, basement, humidity, problem]
---

Hygrometer reads 62 to 68 percent from June through September. Above 60 is
where mold gets comfortable. The smell follows the number.

Three contributors, in order:

1. Surface water. The grade on the north side slopes toward the house and
   the [gutters](/house/gutter-guards.md) overflow in a hard rain.
2. No vapor barrier on the slab.
3. Summer air. Opening the windows in July makes it worse, not better,
   because warm humid air condenses on cool walls.

Doing now: 50-pint dehumidifier draining to the
[sump](/house/sump-pump.md), set to 50 percent. Regrade the north side in
spring.

Top of [the 2026 list](/house/projects-2026.md).
