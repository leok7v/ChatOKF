Thirty square feet of 3x6 white subway tile, running bond, from the counter
to the underside of the cabinets.

The work is not the tiling, it is the layout: find the center, dry-lay the
first course, and decide where the cut tiles land before any adhesive comes
out. Cuts at the ends, never in the middle.

Rent a wet saw for the day. Nippers are for two or three cuts, not thirty.

Premixed mastic for a backsplash, not thinset. Unsanded grout for joints
under an eighth of an inch.

Outlets need box extenders once the tile adds depth. See
[the GFCI note](/house/gfci-outlets.md).
