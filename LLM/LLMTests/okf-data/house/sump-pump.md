title: The sump pump
description: A pedestal pump that runs constantly in spring, with no

---

Pedestal pump in the northeast corner, discharging to daylight about
fifteen feet from the foundation.

It runs every few minutes for most of March. That is normal for the water
table here and it is also why
[the basement humidity](/house/basement-humidity.md) is what it is.

No battery backup. A spring storm that takes the power out is exactly when
the pump is needed, so this is the obvious gap. A battery backup unit is
a few hundred dollars.

The dehumidifier drains into the sump, which saves emptying a bucket.

Test it by pouring a bucket of water in. Twice a year.
