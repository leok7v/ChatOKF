Sixteen feet of shelving, four levels, 24 inches deep, along the back wall.

Sized to the storage bins, which are 16 inches tall, so the shelf spacing
is 18 and not whatever fell out of the lumber dimensions.

Ledger board lagged into the studs at the back, two-by-four frames, 3/4
plywood tops. Nothing freestanding, nothing to tip.

Bottom shelf is 24 inches off the floor so the snow blower and the
dehumidifier fit underneath.
