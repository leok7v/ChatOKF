title: The back door sticks in summer
description: Seasonal swelling, not a failing hinge, so the fix is
  planing a sixteenth off the latch stile in July.
tags: [house, doors, seasonal, problem]
---

Sticks June through September, swings free in winter. That is wood moving
with humidity, not the house settling.

Diagnosis: close it on a strip of paper at several heights and see where it
binds. Ours binds at the top of the latch side.

Fix in July when it is at its worst, so you do not take off too much: plane
a sixteenth off the top of the latch stile, prime and paint the bare edge
immediately or it drinks moisture and swells more.

Tightening the top hinge screws first is free and sometimes enough. Use
three-inch screws into the framing.
