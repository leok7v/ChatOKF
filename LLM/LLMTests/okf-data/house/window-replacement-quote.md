Fourteen double-hung, vinyl, double pane with low-E and argon. Quoted
18,400 dollars installed, January 2025.

Claimed energy savings were around 200 dollars a year, which is a 90-year
payback. The honest reasons to do it are comfort, noise and not fighting
sash cords, not the energy bill.

[Attic insulation](/house/attic-insulation.md) is a fraction of the price
and saves more.

Deferred. The quote is a year old and prices have moved, so this number is
no longer usable.
