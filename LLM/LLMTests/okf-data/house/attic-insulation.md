Measured about six inches of old fiberglass, call it R-19. Recommendation
for this climate is R-49, which is around sixteen inches of blown
cellulose.

Air sealing comes first and matters more: top plates, the bath fan, the
chimney chase, recessed lights, the attic hatch. Insulation over an unsealed
ceiling just filters the air on its way through.

Related: the [bath fan](/house/bathroom-fan.md) currently dumps into this
space.

Blown cellulose, rented blower, two people, one long day. Do not bury the
soffit vents; use baffles.
