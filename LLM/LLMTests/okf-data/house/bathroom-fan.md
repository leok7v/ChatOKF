type: Project
title: Replacing the upstairs bathroom fan
description: The old fan is undersized and vents into the attic, which
  is half of why there is frost on the roof sheathing.
tags: [house, bathroom, ventilation, problem]
---

The existing fan is 50 CFM for a bathroom that needs 80, and it terminates
in the attic rather than through the roof. Warm wet air into a cold attic
becomes frost on the sheathing, which is exactly what
[the insulation note](/house/attic-insulation.md) describes.

Plan: 110 CFM unit with a humidity sensor, insulated duct, rigid, run to a
roof cap. Not a soffit vent, which pulls the same air straight back in.

Sized by room volume plus a bump for the shower. The sensor matters more
than the rating because nobody runs the fan long enough by hand.
