---
type: Procedure
title: Overseeding the lawn in fall
description: Late August through September, which is the only time it
  reliably works in a cool-season lawn.
tags: [house, lawn, yard, procedure]
---

Cool-season grass here, so fall is the window: warm soil, cooler air, less
weed competition. Spring seeding fights crabgrass and loses.

Mow short, dethatch or core aerate, spread seed, starter fertilizer, then
keep the surface damp for two to three weeks. The watering is the part that
decides it.

Do not apply crabgrass preventer within eight weeks either side. It stops
grass seed from germinating too.

Tall fescue blend for the sunny front, fine fescue for the shade under the
maples where nothing else has ever worked.
