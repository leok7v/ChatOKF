type: Note
title: Gutter guards, and which kind actually worked
description: Micro-mesh guards over the two gutters under the maples,
  after the foam inserts failed within a season.
tags: [house, gutters, maintenance]
---

Two runs sit under maples and fill with seed and leaf every year.

Foam inserts: failed. They clog with the fine stuff and then hold it wet
against the gutter.

Brush inserts: same, worse.

Micro-mesh screwed to the gutter lip, not slid under the shingles: works.
Three years in and I have cleaned them once. Stainless mesh, not aluminum.

This feeds directly into
[the basement humidity problem](/house/basement-humidity.md), because an
overflowing gutter dumps at the foundation.
