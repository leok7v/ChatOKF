Short-term charcoal canister in the basement, 48 hours, closed-house
conditions, spring 2024. Result 2.7 picocuries per liter.

The EPA action level is 4.0. Between 2 and 4 they suggest considering
mitigation. Below 2 is where they stop talking.

Short-term tests are noisy and radon varies by season, generally higher in
winter when the house is closed up. A long-term alpha track test over
three months is the honest measurement and I have not done one.

If it ever needs mitigation, sub-slab depressurization ties in near
[the sump](/house/sump-pump.md).
