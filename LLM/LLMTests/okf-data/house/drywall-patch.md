---
type: Procedure
title: Patching drywall
description: Three thin coats with progressively wider knives, sanded
  between, which takes three days and cannot be compressed.
tags: [house, drywall, repair, procedure]
---

Small holes: self-adhesive mesh patch. Anything over four inches: cut a
square, back it with scrap wood screwed inside, fit a new piece.

Then three coats of compound, 6-inch knife, then 10, then 12, each one
wider than the last and each one thin. Dry overnight between. Three days.

The universal mistake is trying to fill it in one thick coat. It cracks and
it leaves a bump you cannot sand flat without hitting paper.

Prime the patch before painting or it flashes through the finish coat. See
[paint colors](/house/paint-colors.md).
