Bosch 800 Series, SHPM88Z75N, installed March 2022. Forty-two decibels,
which means you cannot tell it is running.

It has no heated dry element. It uses a zeolite condensation system, which
is why plastics come out damp and why you must use rinse aid rather than
treat it as optional.

The filter in the bottom unscrews and needs rinsing monthly. Ours went a
year before I learned that, which is why things smelled.

Third rack is for utensils and it is the reason to buy this over the 300
series.
