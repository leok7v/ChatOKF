Two-stage, 24-inch, electric start.

It failed to start two Decembers running. Both times the carburetor was
gummed from ethanol fuel sitting all summer.

Now: ethanol-free fuel only, stabilizer in every can, and run the tank dry
in April rather than storing it full.

Shear pins are consumable and there are spares taped inside the cover. They
are supposed to break; that is what they are for.

Lives under [the garage shelving](/house/garage-shelving.md).
