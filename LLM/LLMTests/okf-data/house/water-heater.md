50-gallon natural gas, installed 2016, in the basement next to the
furnace. Typical life is 8 to 12 years, so it is on the clock.

Never checked the anode rod, which is the sacrificial magnesium rod that
corrodes so the tank does not. Checking it at year six would have been the
cheap way to double the life. Too late to matter much now.

Flush a few gallons off the bottom twice a year for sediment. I do this
about half the years.

When it goes: tankless needs a bigger gas line and a new vent, so the
honest comparison is not just the unit price.
