title: GFCI outlets and where they are required
description: Kitchen, bathrooms, garage, basement and outdoors, and one
  upstream device can protect a whole string.
tags: [house, electrical, safety]
---

Required within six feet of a sink, and in garages, unfinished basements,
crawl spaces and outdoors.

The one thing worth knowing: a single GFCI receptacle wired to protect its
downstream outlets covers the whole string. So the dead outlet in the
garage is usually not dead, it is tripped at a device somewhere else,
often in a bathroom.

Ours: the outdoor outlets are fed from the GFCI in the garage, which is why
they died when the Christmas lights got wet.

Test them monthly with the button. Nobody does. They fail closed.

Relevant to [the backsplash](/house/kitchen-backsplash.md) because of box
depth.
