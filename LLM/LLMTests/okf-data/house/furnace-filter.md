description: A 16x25x4 media filter every six months, and the cheap
  one-inch filters were making the blower work harder for nothing.
tags: [house, hvac, maintenance, schedule]
---

16x25x4 media cabinet, MERV 11, changed at the spring and fall time
changes so I do not have to remember a date.

MERV 13 is better filtration and more restriction. On an older blower that
trade is not free, and a restricted return is how coils freeze.

The one-inch pleated filters that came with the house needed changing every
month and nobody ever did, which is worse than a lower rating changed on
time.
