Plumber, 2023, replaced the main shutoff. Fast, fair, answers the phone.
Call again.

Electrician, 2022, added the basement circuits. Good work, six weeks to
schedule, so plan ahead.

Roofer, 2021 estimate only. Quoted high and pushed hard for a same-day
signature. Did not use, would not call.

HVAC, annual service contract. Adequate. The service contract is probably
not worth what it costs against calling for a visit.

Rule learned the expensive way: three written quotes, itemized, and never
the one who needs an answer today. Applies to
[the window quote](/house/window-replacement-quote.md).
