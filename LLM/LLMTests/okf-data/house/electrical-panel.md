200 amp main, 40 spaces, in the basement by the stairs.

Traced every circuit with a plug-in tester and a helper on the phone, and
relabeled. The original labels had the kitchen and the dining room
reversed and called two different circuits "bedroom".

Notable: the kitchen has two 20-amp small appliance circuits as required,
and the [dishwasher](/house/dishwasher-bosch.md) is on its own.

Four open spaces. Enough for a future car charger, though the service load
calculation is the real question, not the spaces.

Photo of the labeled panel is in the house folder.
