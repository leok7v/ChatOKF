Last done spring 2023. Overdue.

Prep: deck cleaner, scrub, rinse, let it dry two full days. Stain on damp
wood fails within a season and there is no shortcut.

Semi-transparent oil-based. Solid stain looks like paint and peels; clear
sealer offers no UV protection and needs redoing annually.

Two people: one cutting in at the rails, one on the field with a pad
applicator. One board end to end, never half a board, or the lap line
shows.
