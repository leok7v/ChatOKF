title: Sweet and hot peppers
description: Peppers want more heat and less water than I keep giving
  them, and they set fruit late.
tags: [garden, peppers, vegetables]
---

Two bell, two poblano, one jalapeno. The poblanos are the ones worth the
space because they go into everything.

Peppers are slower than tomatoes and need warmer soil. Nothing happens
until nights stay above 55F. I have twice been convinced they were dead in
June and had a full harvest in September.

They want less water than the tomatoes sharing the bed, which is the
awkward part of the [2026 plan](/garden/plan-2026.md).

Poblanos get roasted and go into [chili](/kitchen/chili.md).
