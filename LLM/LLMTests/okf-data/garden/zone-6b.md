USDA hardiness zone 6b: average annual extreme minimum temperature of -5
to 0 degrees Fahrenheit.

What it decides: anything rated to zone 6 or lower stays out.
[Rosemary](/garden/rosemary.md) is rated 7 and does not. Thyme, oregano,
chives, mint and garlic all overwinter fine.

The zone was redrawn warmer in the 2023 map. We used to be 6a.
