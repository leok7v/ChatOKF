Average last spring frost here is about May 10. Average first fall frost is
about October 10. That is a 150-day season.

Average means half the years are later. I have been burned on May 14. For
tender things like [basil](/garden/basil.md) I wait until Memorial Day and
lose nothing.

Counting backward from May 10 is how
[seed starting](/garden/seed-starting.md) is scheduled.
