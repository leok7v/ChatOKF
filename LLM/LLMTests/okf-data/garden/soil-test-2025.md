Sent samples from all three beds to the county extension office in March
2025. Nine dollars, three weeks.

- pH 6.2, slightly acidic, fine for tomatoes and peppers.
- Nitrogen low. Organic matter 4.1 percent.
- Phosphorus and potassium both in the sufficient range.

Recommendation was blood meal or more [compost](/garden/compost.md) for
nitrogen and no lime.

This needs redoing. The numbers drift and these are a year old.
