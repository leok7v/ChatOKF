type: Note
title: Planting garlic in October
description: Hardneck garlic goes in in October, sits through winter,
  and comes out the following July.
tags: [garden, garlic, fall, schedule]
---

Hardneck, because softneck wants a milder winter. Cloves pointy end up,
two inches deep, six inches apart, sometime in the second half of October.
Mulch with straw after the ground gets cold.

It sends up green in November, looks dead in January, and takes off in
March.

Cut the scapes in June, which makes bigger bulbs and gives you scapes.
Harvest when the bottom three leaves brown, usually mid-July. Goes into
bed C after [the lettuce](/garden/lettuce-succession.md).
