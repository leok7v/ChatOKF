description: Keeping a few basil plants alive indoors through winter,
  and why they never taste as good as the summer ones.
tags: [garden, herbs, basil, indoor]
---

Four small pots on the kitchen sill, south facing. They survive the winter
but they are not the same plant as the ones outside: leggy, thinner leaves,
much milder. Not enough light, basically.

What actually helps is a cheap clip-on grow light on a timer for six hours
a day. Without it they stretch toward the glass and fall over by February.

Water less than you think. Indoor basil dies of wet roots far more often
than of thirst. Same plant, different problem from
[the outdoor basil](/garden/basil.md).
