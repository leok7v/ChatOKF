title: Early blight on the tomatoes
description: The brown ringed spots that climb from the bottom leaves
  up, what causes it, and what actually slowed it down.
tags: [garden, tomatoes, disease, problem]
---

It starts on the lowest leaves as brown spots with faint concentric rings,
yellow halo around them, and works upward. By August the bottom foot of the
plant is bare sticks.

It is soil-borne and it splashes up when it rains. Three things helped, in
order of how much they helped:

1. Mulch. Straw over the soil stops the splash. Biggest single win.
2. Strip the bottom 12 inches of leaves once the plant is established.
3. Wider spacing, see [tomatoes](/garden/tomatoes.md).

Do not compost the infected leaves. They go in the trash, not
[the pile](/garden/compost.md).
