Three cedar beds, each 4 x 8 feet and 12 inches deep, on the south side
where they get sun from about 9am to 6pm. Cedar because it lasts without
anything painted on it.

Filled roughly half screened topsoil, a quarter
[compost](/garden/compost.md), a quarter coarse sand. They settled about
two inches the first winter and I top them up with compost every spring.

The wood is starting to check on the south faces after three seasons.
Not rot, just cracks. I expect to rebuild around 2029.
