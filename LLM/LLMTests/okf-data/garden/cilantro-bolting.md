type: Note
title: Cilantro bolts the moment it is warm
description: Cilantro goes to seed in early summer no matter what, so I
  sow it in spring and fall and stop fighting it.
tags: [garden, herbs, cilantro, problem]
---

Every year I plant cilantro in May and every year it is a flower stalk by
mid-June. It is a cool-season plant and heat triggers bolting. Slow-bolt
varieties buy about ten days.

What works: sow in March and again in September, and let the summer batch
bolt on purpose. The seed is coriander, which is worth having anyway.

Goes into [tacos al pastor](/kitchen/tacos-al-pastor.md) and the
[mole](/kitchen/mole-negro.md).
