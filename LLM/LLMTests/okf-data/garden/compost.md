description: A two-bin pile behind the garage, the brown-to-green ratio
  that keeps it from smelling, and what never goes in.
tags: [garden, compost, soil]
---

Two bins behind the garage: one being filled, one finishing. Roughly three
parts brown to one part green by volume, where brown is leaves and
cardboard and green is kitchen scraps and grass.

If it smells sour it is too wet and too green. Add leaves and turn it.
If nothing is happening it is too dry, or too small to hold heat.

Never: meat, dairy, oil, pet waste, or anything off the
[blighted tomatoes](/garden/tomato-blight.md).

Finished compost tops up [the beds](/garden/raised-beds.md) every spring.
