title: Flat-leaf parsley
description: Parsley is a biennial, so the second-year plants bolt early
  and the first-year ones are the ones to pick from.
tags: [garden, herbs, parsley]
---

Flat-leaf, not curly. Curly is garnish, flat is food.

It is a biennial: year one it makes leaves, year two it flowers and is
done. So a plant that overwinters looks like a free head start and then
bolts in May. Start new seed every spring.

Germination is slow, two to three weeks, and it looks like nothing is
happening. Soak the seed overnight.
