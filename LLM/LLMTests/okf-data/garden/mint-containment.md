type: Note
title: Keeping mint from taking the bed
description: Mint spreads by runners and will own a raised bed in two
  seasons, so it stays in a buried pot.
tags: [garden, herbs, mint, problem]
---

I planted mint directly in bed C in 2023 and spent 2024 pulling it out of
the lettuce. It travels by underground runners, and any fragment left
behind becomes a new plant.

Now it lives in a plastic nursery pot sunk into the ground with the rim
two inches proud of the soil, because it will also jump over a flush rim.

Worth it for mojitos and for the mint in [bun cha](/kitchen/bun-cha.md).
