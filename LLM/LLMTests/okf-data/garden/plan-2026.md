Three beds again, same layout as last year except the tomatoes and the
squash swap sides so the tomatoes are not following themselves.

- Bed A: [tomatoes](/garden/tomatoes.md), basil tucked in at the ends.
- Bed B: [peppers](/garden/peppers.md) and
  [zucchini](/garden/zucchini.md).
- Bed C: [lettuce on succession](/garden/lettuce-succession.md), then
  fall [garlic](/garden/garlic-fall-planting.md).

Changes for this year: run the [drip line](/garden/drip-irrigation.md)
before anything goes in the ground instead of after, start seeds two weeks
later than I think I should, and actually stake the tomatoes in May rather
than wrestling them in July.

Everything keys off [our last frost date](/garden/last-frost.md).
