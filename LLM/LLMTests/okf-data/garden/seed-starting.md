Counting back from [last frost](/garden/last-frost.md) on May 10:

- Week of March 25: tomatoes and peppers into cells under lights.
- Lights two inches above the leaves, 14 hours, raised as they grow.
- Bottom water. Top watering knocks seedlings over and invites damping off.
- Week of April 29: start hardening off.
- Week of May 20: plant out.

Hardening off is one hour outside in shade on day one, adding an hour and
more sun each day for a week. Skip it and the leaves bleach white in two
days. I have done this twice.
