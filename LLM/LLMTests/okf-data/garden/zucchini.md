type: Note
title: Zucchini, and how two plants is one too many
description: Two plants produce more zucchini than any household can
  eat, and powdery mildew arrives every August regardless.
tags: [garden, squash, vegetables]
---

One plant. One. Two plants means standing in the driveway trying to give
zucchini to neighbors who are also growing zucchini.

Powdery mildew shows up in August every single year, white dust on the
upper leaves. It does not kill the plant, it just ends the season early.
Airflow helps, overhead watering makes it worse.

Pick them at 6 to 8 inches. The baseball-bat ones are watery and seedy and
only good grated into [banana bread](/kitchen/banana-bread.md), which is a
thing I have actually done.
