Four plants, two Sungold and two Better Boy, which covers both
"eat them off the vine" and "cook them down".

Tomatoes are technically a berry and they ripen green to red on their own
schedule no matter what I do. What I control is air and water.

Space them 30 inches apart, not 24. At 24 the leaves never dry out and that
is how [blight](/garden/tomato-blight.md) starts. Florida weave between
steel stakes beats cages for anything indeterminate.

Water at the soil, never over the leaves. The
[drip line](/garden/drip-irrigation.md) handles that.

Everything that does not get eaten fresh becomes
[sauce](/kitchen/tomato-sauce.md).
