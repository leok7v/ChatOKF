Sungold. Orange, absurdly sweet, and the plant produces until frost.

The one failure mode is splitting: a dry week followed by a soaking and the
skins crack right around the shoulders. Even watering is the whole fix,
which is the argument for the [drip line](/garden/drip-irrigation.md) being
on a timer rather than on me remembering.

They almost never make it to the kitchen. See
[tomatoes](/garden/tomatoes.md) for the ones that do.
