---
type: Note
title: Succession planting lettuce
description: Sowing a short row every two weeks instead of all at once,
  so there is lettuce for months rather than a wall of it in June.
tags: [garden, lettuce, greens, technique]
---

A three-foot row every two weeks from four weeks before
[last frost](/garden/last-frost.md) until the end of May, then stop until
late August for a fall run.

Lettuce bolts when it gets hot: the head stretches upward, sends up a
flower stalk and the leaves turn bitter within days. Nothing reverses it.
Shade cloth buys maybe two weeks in July.

Loose-leaf types last longer than heads and you can cut and come again.
