title: Slugs in the lettuce
description: Slugs eat the lettuce overnight and the only things that
  worked were beer traps and not mulching that bed.
tags: [garden, pests, problem, lettuce]
---

Ragged holes in the lettuce, no insect visible during the day, silvery
trails in the morning. Slugs.

What worked: a shallow dish of beer sunk to soil level, emptied every
couple of days, and pulling the straw mulch off bed C. The mulch that
protects [the tomatoes](/garden/tomato-blight.md) is a slug hotel in the
greens bed.

What did not work: crushed eggshells, coffee grounds, copper tape.
