---
type: Note
title: Thyme and oregano
description: The two herbs that survive on neglect, come back every
  year, and want poor dry soil.
tags: [garden, herbs, perennial]
---

Both are Mediterranean, both are perennial here, both do better in the
gravelly strip by the walk than they ever did in the good soil of
[the raised beds](/garden/raised-beds.md). Rich soil makes them soft and
flavorless.

Cut them back hard in spring. Oregano especially will sprawl into a woody
mat if you let it.

Oregano dried is stronger than fresh, which is backwards from most herbs.
