title: Growing basil
description: Basil hates cold, bolts the moment it flowers, and wants
  pinching every week to stay bushy.
tags: [garden, herbs, basil]

Basil is a tender tropical plant, originally from India, and it behaves
like one. Below about 50F it sulks and the leaves go papery and dark. I do
not put it out until well after [last frost](/garden/last-frost.md), which
in practice means late May.

The whole trick is pinching. Take the growing tip off above a leaf pair
every week or so and it branches instead of shooting up. The moment you let
it flower the leaves turn small and bitter and it is over.

Six plants keeps us in [pesto](/kitchen/pesto.md) all summer with enough
left for [caprese](/kitchen/caprese.md). I also keep a few in the
[window box](/garden/basil-window-box.md) for winter.
