---
type: Note
title: How much to water
description: An inch a week including rain, delivered deeply twice
  rather than lightly every day.
tags: [garden, water, technique]
---

About an inch a week total. A shallow daily sprinkle grows shallow roots
and those are the plants that collapse in the first hot week.

Two deep soaks beat seven light ones. I put a tuna can out to measure what
the [drip line](/garden/drip-irrigation.md) actually delivers, which turned
out to be half what I assumed.

Morning, not evening. Wet leaves overnight is how
[blight](/garden/tomato-blight.md) spreads.
