  and comes into the garage over winter.

In [zone 6b](/garden/zone-6b.md) rosemary is a coin flip outdoors. Mine
lives in a 14-inch terracotta pot that goes into the unheated garage in
November, near the window, watered maybe once a month.

It wants to be dry. Every rosemary I have killed, I drowned.

Comes back out in April. Goes on the
[roast chicken](/kitchen/roast-chicken.md) and into the focaccia.
