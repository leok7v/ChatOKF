title: The deer fence that is not tall enough
description: Six feet of welded wire keeps the deer out most of the year
  and does not in a hard winter.
tags: [garden, deer, fence, problem]
---

Six-foot welded wire on T-posts around the three beds. Deer can clear eight
feet if they are motivated, and in February they are motivated.

Two winters out of three it holds. The third they came over and took the
kale to the stems.

The fix is either two more feet, or a second fence three feet outside the
first, because deer are bad at judging depth. I have not done either.
