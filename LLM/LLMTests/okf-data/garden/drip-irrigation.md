Half-inch mainline from the hose bib down the path, quarter-inch emitter
tubing looped through each bed, hose-bib timer at the spigot. Under eighty
dollars of parts.

Runs 30 minutes at 6am, three days a week, adjusted up in July. See
[watering](/garden/watering.md) for how I checked the actual delivery.

Two things I got wrong the first time: I ran it after planting, so I was
threading tube around live plants, and I did not put a filter in. The
emitters clogged by August.

This is also what keeps [cherry tomatoes](/garden/cherry-tomatoes.md) from
splitting.
