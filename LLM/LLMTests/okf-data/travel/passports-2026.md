Both passports expire in 2029. Many countries require six months of
validity beyond the date of entry, so that is fine until 2028.

Visa situation for the planned list, as of early 2026:

- Schengen: visa free for 90 days. ETIAS, the pre-travel authorization,
  has been announced and delayed repeatedly. Check before
  [Portugal](/travel/portugal.md).
- Mexico, Peru, Chile, Argentina, Colombia, Costa Rica: visa free.
- Japan and South Korea: visa free. Korea has a K-ETA requirement that has
  been waived and reinstated more than once.
- Vietnam: e-visa required, apply online, about a week.
- Thailand: visa free for 60 days as of 2024.

These change. Reverify before booking anything.
