  Americas and food at every price level.

Roma Norte and Condesa to stay, Centro and Coyoacan to walk.

The Museo Nacional de Antropologia is the reason to come even if you do not
like museums. Half a day minimum.

It sits at 7,350 feet. The first day is slower than you expect and alcohol
hits harder.

Tacos al pastor from a trompo on a street corner at midnight, which is what
[my sheet-pan version](/kitchen/tacos-al-pastor.md) is chasing.

Teotihuacan is an hour out, go early.

Pairs with [Oaxaca](/travel/oaxaca.md).
