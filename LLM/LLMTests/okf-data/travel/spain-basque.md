[San Sebastian](/travel/san-sebastian.md) for the food, Bilbao for the
Guggenheim and because it is a real working city, and the coast road
between them for the drive.

Basque is not a dialect of Spanish. It is not related to Spanish, or to
any other living language. Signs are in both.

Late September: the weather holds, the crowds thin, and the cider houses
are open.

On [the bucket list](/travel/bucket-list.md) for 2026.
