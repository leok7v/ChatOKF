title: The Greek islands, but not the famous ones
description: Naxos and the smaller Cyclades instead of Santorini, for a
  quarter of the cost and none of the cruise ships.
tags: [travel, europe, greece, islands]
---

Santorini and Mykonos are expensive and full. Naxos, Milos, Folegandros
and Sifnos are not, and the ferries connect them.

Ferries are the whole logistics problem. Book them, they sell out in
August, and build in a spare day because wind cancels them.

Naxos has the best food of the Cyclades and an actual working agricultural
economy, which is why.
