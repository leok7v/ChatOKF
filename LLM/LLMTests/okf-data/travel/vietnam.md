Hanoi four nights, Ha Long or better Lan Ha bay overnight, Hoi An three,
Saigon three, flying between rather than the long train unless the train
is the point.

Hanoi is where [bun cha](/kitchen/bun-cha.md) comes from and it is a
lunch dish, sold out by two.

North and south are different cuisines. Pho in Hanoi is clear and plain;
in Saigon it arrives with a plate of herbs and hoisin.

Weather never works for the whole country at once. October and November are
the least bad compromise.

On [the Asia list](/travel/bucket-list.md).
