Kyoto was the capital of Japan from 794 until 1868 and it kept the
temples when Tokyo took the government. About 1.5 million people, in the
Kansai region.

The entire strategy is time of day. Fushimi Inari at six in the morning is
empty and silent. At ten it is a queue. Same for Arashiyama bamboo and
Kiyomizu-dera.

Four days. Higashiyama on foot one day, Arashiyama another, Fushimi and
the south a third, and one day with nothing planned.

Nara is 45 minutes away and is a half day.

Pairs with [Tokyo](/travel/japan-tokyo.md) on
[the Asia trip](/travel/bucket-list.md).
