title: Packing carry-on only
description: Two weeks in a 40 litre bag, which works because of laundry
  rather than because of clever folding.
tags: [travel, packing, procedure]
---

40 litre bag, personal item, nothing checked.

Four shirts, two pants, five of everything else, one jacket, one pair of
shoes plus the ones on your feet. That is a week. Two weeks is the same
list plus doing laundry once, in a sink or a laundromat, which takes an
afternoon you would have spent in a museum you did not want to see anyway.

Liquids are the binding constraint, not clothes. Bar shampoo removes the
problem.

The bag must fit the smallest airline on the itinerary, which in Europe is
usually the budget carrier and is smaller than the American rule.
