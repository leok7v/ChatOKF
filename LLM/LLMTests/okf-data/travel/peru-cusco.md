title: Cusco and Machu Picchu
description: Altitude is the planning constraint, and the permits for
  the Inca Trail sell out six months ahead.
tags: [travel, latin-america, peru, hiking]
---

Cusco is at 11,150 feet. Two days there doing nothing before any hiking.
Coca tea helps a little, acclimatization helps entirely.

Inca Trail permits: 500 a day including guides and porters, released in
October for the following year, gone within days. The Salkantay and Lares
routes do not need them.

Or the train to Aguas Calientes, which is what most people do, and there is
no shame in it.

Sacred Valley is lower than Cusco and a better place to sleep the first
night. Ollantaytambo specifically.

Dry season May to September.
