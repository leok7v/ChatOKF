title: Sicily
description: A ten-day loop by car: Palermo, the Greek temples, Siracusa
  and Etna, in spring or October.
tags: [travel, europe, italy, sicily]
---

Fly into Palermo, out of Catania, rent a car, drive the loop. Ten days.

Palermo markets, Segesta and Agrigento for the Greek temples which are
better preserved than most of what is in Greece, Siracusa and Ortigia,
then Etna.

Sicily was Greek before it was Roman and Arab before it was Norman, and all
four are visible in the same street.

April or October. August is brutal and the coast is booked.
