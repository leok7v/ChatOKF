  driving than the map suggests.

Monteverde cloud forest, Arenal volcano, and one coast. Pick one coast.
Trying to do both Pacific and Caribbean in ten days is all driving.

The roads are slow. Forty miles can be two and a half hours. Budget by
hours, not by distance, and do not drive at night.

December to April is dry season. September and October are the wettest
months on the Pacific side and the best on the Caribbean.

Rent a 4x4 for Monteverde specifically.
