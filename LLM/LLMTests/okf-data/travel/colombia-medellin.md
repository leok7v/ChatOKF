title: Medellin
description: Spring weather year round, a city rebuilt around its metro
  and cable cars, and cheap.
tags: [travel, latin-america, colombia, city]
---

Seventy-five degrees every day of the year, which is why they call it the
city of eternal spring. 4,900 feet and near the equator.

The metro and the cable cars up the hillsides are the story: public
transport as the deliberate instrument of turning the city around after the
nineties.

Comuna 13 with a guide, not alone, and understand what you are looking at.

Guatape is a day trip. The rock is 700 steps.

Cheap enough that a two-week trip costs what four days in
[Norway](/travel/norway-fjords.md) does.
