type: Note
title: Seoul
description: A dense, late-night city with mountains inside it and the
  best public transport of the three Asian capitals.
tags: [travel, asia, korea, city]
---

Bukchon and Seochon for the old streets, Hongdae and Euljiro for the
evening, Bukhansan for a mountain inside the city limits.

The metro is signed in English, runs late, and the T-money card works on
everything including taxis.

Korean barbecue is a communal meal and the side dishes are free and
refilled.

Jimjilbang, the bathhouse, is the thing to do on a cold afternoon.

Would combine with [Tokyo](/travel/japan-tokyo.md) on a longer Asia trip.
