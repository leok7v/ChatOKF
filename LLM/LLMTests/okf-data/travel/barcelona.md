La Sagrada Familia is worth it and you must book a timed entry weeks out.
The towers are a separate ticket. Park Guell likewise.

Barcelona has had genuine anti-tourism protests. Behave accordingly: stay
in a hotel rather than an apartment, eat dinner at nine like everyone else,
and do not treat the Boqueria as a lunch counter.

Catalan, not Spanish, is the first language on the signs.

Not currently on [the list](/travel/bucket-list.md); we have been.
