Not a ranking. An ordering by season, cost and how long we can be away.

Europe, probably 2026 and 2027:
[Portugal](/travel/portugal.md), [the Basque country](/travel/spain-basque.md),
[Sicily](/travel/sicily.md), [Iceland](/travel/iceland.md).

Latin America, one long trip:
[Oaxaca](/travel/oaxaca.md), [Mexico City](/travel/mexico-city.md),
[Cusco and Machu Picchu](/travel/peru-cusco.md),
[Patagonia](/travel/patagonia.md).

Asia, the big one, three weeks minimum:
[Kyoto](/travel/japan-kyoto.md), [Tokyo](/travel/japan-tokyo.md),
[Vietnam](/travel/vietnam.md),
[Chiang Mai](/travel/thailand-chiang-mai.md).

Constraints: two weeks at a time unless we combine vacation and the week
between Christmas and New Year. See
[points and miles](/travel/points-and-miles.md) for how we pay for it.
