  and a rail system that makes all of them easy.

Pick neighborhoods, not sights. Shimokitazawa, Yanaka, Koenji, Nakameguro
are worth more days than the Shibuya crossing.

Suica card on the phone, tap through everything, stop thinking about it.

The JR Pass stopped being automatically worth it after the 2023 price
increase. Do the arithmetic for the actual route.

Food at every level: the basement food halls of the department stores are
as good as anything and cost nothing.

Four days, plus [Kyoto](/travel/japan-kyoto.md).
