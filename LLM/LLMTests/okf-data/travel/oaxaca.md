Oaxaca is a state in southeast Mexico and the city is Oaxaca de Juarez. It
has more speakers of indigenous languages than any other Mexican state,
mostly Zapotec and Mixtec, and that is audible in the market.

The food is the reason: seven moles, tlayudas, chapulines, quesillo,
and mezcal made in the villages out in the valleys rather than in a
factory.

We took a cooking class in a market kitchen, which is where the notes for
[mole negro](/kitchen/mole-negro.md) came from.

Day of the Dead at the start of November is extraordinary and the city is
completely full. Book six months out or go another time.

Monte Alban, the Zapotec site above the city, half a day.

Pairs with [Mexico City](/travel/mexico-city.md) on the same trip.
