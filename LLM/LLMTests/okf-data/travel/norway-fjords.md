Bergen, the Bergen to Oslo railway, the Flam branch down to the fjord, and
a boat through Naeroyfjord. That is the classic loop and it works in four
days.

It rains in Bergen. It rains 240 days a year in Bergen.

Very expensive, on the order of Iceland. Same strategy: groceries.

Summer is midnight sun and crowds. Late May is green, empty and still
light until eleven.
