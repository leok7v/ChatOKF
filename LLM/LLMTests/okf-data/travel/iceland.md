title: Iceland in the shoulder season
description: The ring road in September, when the roads are open, the
  nights are dark enough for aurora, and the prices drop.
tags: [travel, europe, iceland, nature]
---

Ring road, ten days, counterclockwise so the south coast is last.

September is the window: the highland roads are still open, it is dark
enough by ten for the northern lights, and both crowds and prices are off
their summer peak.

Everything is expensive. Groceries from Bonus, not restaurants, for most
meals.

Rent the car with gravel and sand insurance. This is not upselling.

Weather closes roads with no notice. Check the road service every morning.
