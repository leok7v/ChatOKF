Alfama, Baixa, Bairro Alto, Belem. Four days without hurrying.

The hills are real. Shoes matter more than any guidebook admits.

Pasteis de nata at Pasteis de Belem, which is the original, and then again
at the bakery on whatever corner you happen to be standing on, which is
usually just as good.

Day trip to Sintra, early, because the buses up to the palaces are
impossible by eleven.

Part of [the Portugal plan](/travel/portugal.md).
