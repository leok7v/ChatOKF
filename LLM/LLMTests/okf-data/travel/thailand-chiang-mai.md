title: Chiang Mai
description: Northern Thailand, cooler than Bangkok, temples and a
  cooking school, and the burning season to avoid.
tags: [travel, asia, thailand, food]
---

Cooler and slower than Bangkok. The old city is a square inside a moat and
you can walk it.

Do not go February through early April. That is the burning season, when
agricultural fires put the air quality among the worst in the world.
November to January is the window.

Khao soi is the northern dish and it exists nowhere else in the same form.

A day at a cooking school here is better value than one anywhere else I
have looked, and they take you to the market first.

Ethical elephant sanctuaries only, meaning no riding, and check who owns
them.
