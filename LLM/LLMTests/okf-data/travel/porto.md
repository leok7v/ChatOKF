  is the actual reason to come.

The city is smaller and steeper than [Lisbon](/travel/lisbon.md) and more
worn in. Two days is enough for the city itself.

The port lodges are in Vila Nova de Gaia, across the Douro on the other
bank, not in Porto proper.

Upstream, the Douro valley is terraced vineyards on slate, and the train
along the river from Porto to Pinhao is one of the good ones. Sit on the
right going up.

Part of [the Portugal plan](/travel/portugal.md).
