description: Two cards, transferable points, and booking international

One transferable-points card for everything, one airline card for the
checked bag and boarding group.

The value is in transfer partners, not in the cash-back rate. Points moved
to an airline program for a long-haul seat are worth several times what
they are worth against a statement.

Award seats open at eleven months and again about two weeks out. The middle
is a desert.

This is what makes [Patagonia](/travel/patagonia.md) possible at all.

Do not chase status. We do not fly enough for it to mean anything.
