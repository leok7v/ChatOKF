description: Ten days from Lisbon to Porto with the Douro valley in

Ten days is enough for [Lisbon](/travel/lisbon.md),
[Porto](/travel/porto.md) and the Douro valley between them, with two
nights somewhere in the middle.

Go in May or late September. July and August are hot and the coast is
full.

The train from Lisbon to Porto is under three hours and there is no reason
to rent a car until the valley.

Language: everyone in the cities speaks English, and almost nobody expects
an American to attempt Portuguese, which makes attempting it
disproportionately effective.
