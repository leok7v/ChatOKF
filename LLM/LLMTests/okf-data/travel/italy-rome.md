title: Rome
description: Four days, everything booked in advance, and an acceptance
  that you are not going to see it all.
tags: [travel, europe, italy, city]
---

Book the Vatican Museums and the Colosseum online, weeks ahead, first slot
of the day. Everything else can be walked to.

The Pantheon is free and is the single most impressive room in the city.

Eat away from the piazzas. Two streets off anything famous, the price drops
by half and the food improves.

Water from the street fountains is drinkable and cold. Bring a bottle.
