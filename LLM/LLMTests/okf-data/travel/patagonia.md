Chilean side for Torres del Paine, Argentine side for El Chalten and Fitz
Roy. They are a long bus ride apart and both are worth it.

Season is November to March. January is high summer and booked.

Refugios in Torres del Paine for the W trek book out in August for the
following season.

The wind runs 60 miles an hour for days at a time. It is not a detail, it
decides whether you hike.

This is the expensive one. Flights to Punta Arenas or El Calafate are most
of the cost. See [points](/travel/points-and-miles.md).
