description: Pintxos are not tapas: you stand, you eat one, you pay, you

The rule is one pintxo and one small drink per bar, then move. Sitting down
and ordering six of them is the tourist mistake and it is also worse food,
because the good ones are made to order from the board behind the bar, not
sitting out on the counter.

Parte Vieja, the old town, is where they are. Start around eight.

Txakoli is the local white, poured from a height, slightly fizzy.

Also: three Michelin three-star restaurants in a city of 190,000 people,
none of which we are going to.
