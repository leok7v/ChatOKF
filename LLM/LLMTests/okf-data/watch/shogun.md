An English pilot shipwrecked in Japan in 1600, in the middle of a
succession crisis.

Most of it is in Japanese with subtitles, which is the decision that makes
it work. The 1980 version kept the English character as the audience's
translator; this one does not, and the Japanese characters are the story.

Watched over three weeks. Requires attention, which means after 8:30. See
[the routines](/person/routines.md).

Season two is on [the watchlist](/watch/watchlist.md).
