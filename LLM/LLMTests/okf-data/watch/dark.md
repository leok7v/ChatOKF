German, subtitled. A missing child in a small town turns into a
time-travel story spanning 1953, 1986, 2019 and later.

It is genuinely complicated. Most people watching it keep a chart of who
is whose parent, and the show earns that rather than being confusing for
effect.

Do not watch it dubbed and do not watch it while doing something else.

Joe alone, on [the watchlist](/watch/watchlist.md). Dana is out.
