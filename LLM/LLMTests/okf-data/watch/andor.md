A prequel about how a rebellion is actually assembled: paperwork, prisons,
meetings, informants.

It works entirely on its own. Joe watched it without Dana having seen a
Star Wars film and she followed it fine.

The prison arc in the middle of season one is the best stretch.

Recommended to people who do not like Star Wars, which is an odd pitch and
an accurate one.
