A poor family insinuates itself into the household of a rich one.

It changes genre about halfway through and the turn is better if nobody
has told you about it.

Won Best Picture in 2020, the first film not in English to do so.

The house itself is the argument: every level of it means something. That
is the second-watch reward.

Korean, subtitled.
