A wealthy novelist dies and everyone in the house had a motive.

Funny, fast, and clean enough that an eleven-year-old can watch it.
That combination is why it comes up so often on
[movie night](/watch/family-movie-night.md).

The sequel is on [the watchlist](/watch/watchlist.md). It is sillier and
still works.
