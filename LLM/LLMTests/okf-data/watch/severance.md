title: Severance
description: Apple TV's office thriller about surgically separating work
  memories from home memories, and the best thing either of us watched.
tags: [watch, show, apple-tv, scifi]
---

The premise: employees at a company undergo a procedure that splits their
memory, so the self at work and the self at home never share an
experience.

The office set does an enormous amount of the work. Long corridors, flat
light, nothing to look at.

Season one is close to perfect. Season two takes longer to get where it is
going and gets there.

Dana liked it more than Joe, which is unusual for anything with a
science-fiction premise.

Highest recommendation on [the list](/watch/watchlist.md).
