The BBC natural history series, narrated by David Attenborough.

It is the compromise choice: nobody objects, it can be joined at any
point, and an episode is fifty minutes.

It is also the only thing in the house that visibly rewards the 4K set,
because most streaming drama is graded dark and compressed.

Ben will actually sit through a whole episode, which puts it in a category
of about three things. See
[family movie night](/watch/family-movie-night.md).
