Currently paying for:

- Netflix, standard with ads. The kids' default.
- Apple TV, which is the smallest catalog and the highest hit rate.
- Amazon Prime Video, which comes with the shipping.
- Hulu, bundled, mostly for FX.

About 55 dollars a month all in.

The theory is to rotate: subscribe for a month, watch the thing, cancel.
In practice nobody cancels anything. Auditing this twice a year would
probably save 200 dollars.

All billed through the account in
[the password manager](/tech/password-manager.md), watched on
[the Apple TV](/tech/appletv.md).
