Saturday after dinner. Rules that emerged by necessity:

- Whoever picks does not get to pick again until everyone has.
- Nothing over two hours on a school-night-adjacent week.
- Ben gets a veto on anything genuinely frightening. He is eleven.
- Phones in the kitchen.

What has worked: [Knives Out](/watch/knives-out.md),
[Planet Earth](/watch/planet-earth.md) episodes,
[Ted Lasso](/watch/ted-lasso.md) for a while,
[Everything Everywhere](/watch/everything-everywhere.md) with Maya only.

What has not: anything subtitled, with Ben. That rules out most of
[the good list](/watch/watchlist.md).

Part of [the weekly rhythm](/person/routines.md).
