title: Succession
description: Four seasons of a media dynasty's children failing to
  replace their father, with nobody to root for and that being the
  point.
tags: [watch, show, hbo, drama]
---

A family that owns a media conglomerate, circling the question of who
takes over.

There is no sympathetic character and the show knows it. What keeps it
watchable is that it is extremely funny, which people who have not seen it
never expect.

The dialogue is the whole thing. Subtitles on, it moves fast.

Ends properly, which is unusual.
