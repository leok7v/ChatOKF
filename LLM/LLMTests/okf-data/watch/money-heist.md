Spanish, original title La Casa de Papel. A heist on the Royal Mint of
Spain, planned by a man called the Professor.

The first two parts are tight and fun. After that it expands into
something much longer and much sillier and the tension never comes back.

Watch the first two parts. Stopping there is a legitimate ending.

Castilian Spanish, which is a different accent from anything picked up in
[Mexico](/travel/mexico-city.md).
