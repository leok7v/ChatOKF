Part one, 2021, part two, 2024.

The scale is the point. It is one of the few things where the
[soundbar](/tech/soundbar.md) genuinely changes the experience, and the
dialogue mixing is famously quiet against the score, so
[night mode](/tech/soundbar.md) actually hurts here. Turn it off for this.

Part two is better than part one and does not work without it.
