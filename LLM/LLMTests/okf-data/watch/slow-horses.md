A dumping ground department for intelligence officers who have made career
ending mistakes, run by a spectacularly unpleasant man who is better at
the job than anyone above him.

Six episodes a season, each season a complete story from one of the novels.
That length is the reason it always gets watched and never abandoned.

Funnier than the premise suggests.

Currently mid season four on [the watchlist](/watch/watchlist.md).
