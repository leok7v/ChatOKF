One chef per episode, slow, gorgeous, with a lot of classical music.

Best watched one at a time with a gap. Three in a row and the structure
becomes obvious: hardship, breakthrough, philosophy, plated close-ups.

The pastry season is the one worth starting with for anyone who
[bakes](/kitchen/croissants.md).

Less interesting than [The Bear](/watch/the-bear.md) on what a kitchen is
actually like, and much prettier.
