title: Squid Game
description: The Korean survival series that became the most-watched
  thing on Netflix, and is more about debt than about the games.
tags: [watch, show, netflix, korean]
---

Hundreds of people in crushing debt compete in children's games for a cash
prize, and losing is fatal.

The violence is the hook and the debt is the subject. It is specifically
about South Korean household debt, which is why it reads differently there.

Subtitles, not the dub. The dub flattens the class markers in how the
characters speak, which is half the characterization.

Related: [Seoul](/travel/south-korea-seoul.md) is on the list partly
because of this and partly because of the food.
