Two children in Seoul are separated when one family emigrates. They
reconnect twelve years later online, and meet in person twelve years after
that.

Almost nothing happens and it is devastating.

Korean and English, subtitled in parts.

The opposite of everything else on this list in volume and it is the one
Dana brings up.
