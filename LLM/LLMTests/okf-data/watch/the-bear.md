A fine-dining chef takes over his late brother's Chicago sandwich shop.

It is very loud and very tense, deliberately. The seventh episode of season
one is one continuous shot of a kitchen falling apart and it is either the
best episode of television in years or unwatchable, depending on who is on
the couch.

Season two is gentler and better. Season three is mostly a mood.

Directly responsible for the [knife sharpening](/kitchen/knife-sharpening.md)
habit.
