type: Note
title: Oppenheimer
description: Nolan's three-hour film about the bomb, which is mostly
  people talking in rooms and is gripping anyway.
tags: [watch, film, historical]
---

Three hours, two timelines, one of them in black and white, and the second
half is a security clearance hearing.

It is a film about consequences rather than about the explosion, and the
explosion is not the climax.

Subtitles help. The sound mix buries dialogue under the score, which is a
standing complaint about this director. See
[the soundbar](/tech/soundbar.md).
