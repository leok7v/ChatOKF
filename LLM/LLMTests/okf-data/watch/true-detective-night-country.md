Two detectives in a northern Alaskan town during the weeks when the sun
does not rise.

The setting does most of the work and it is worth it for that alone.

People who wanted season one again were annoyed. Watched on its own terms
it is good.

The dark is genuinely oppressive on screen, which is the point and also
means the [TV settings](/tech/tv-calibration.md) matter. Vivid mode
destroys it.
