Season one is a tight, generous comedy about decency. It is the one
everyone means when they recommend it.

Season two is uneven and season three is too long, and neither undoes the
first.

The one show on this list all four of us watched together, which is rarer
than it sounds. See [family movie night](/watch/family-movie-night.md).
