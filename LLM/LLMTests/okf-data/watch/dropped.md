Abandoned, with the reason, because otherwise one of us adds it back to
[the watchlist](/watch/watchlist.md) in a year.

- A prestige fantasy series, two episodes. Too many names too fast.
- A streaming sci-fi series, four episodes. Good premise, no plot.
- [Money Heist](/watch/money-heist.md) after part two, deliberately.
- A true crime documentary series, one episode. Exploitative.
- A long-running network procedural, three episodes. Fine, and there are
  two hundred of them.

The pattern: anything that needs more than three episodes to become good
does not survive a 6:30 dinner and an 8:30 start.
