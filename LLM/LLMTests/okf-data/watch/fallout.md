Another game adaptation, this one played for satire: a retro-futuristic
America two hundred years after a nuclear war, with a vault dweller coming
up to the surface.

It is gory and it is a comedy, and it holds both.

Lighter than [The Last of Us](/watch/the-last-of-us.md) doing similar
material.
