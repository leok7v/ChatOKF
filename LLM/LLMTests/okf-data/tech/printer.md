Monochrome laser, network, on a
[DHCP reservation](/tech/router-settings.md).

The reasoning: we print maybe twenty pages a month, mostly school forms
and shipping labels. An inkjet that sits idle clogs its heads and the
cartridges dry out, so every print costs a cleaning cycle. A laser sits for
six months and prints.

Toner has lasted three years. Color goes to a print shop about twice a
year, which is cheaper than owning color.

Scanning happens on a phone, not on the printer.
