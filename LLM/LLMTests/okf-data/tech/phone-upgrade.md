type: Note
title: Phone upgrade thinking
description: Keeping phones four years instead of two, since the only
  thing that actually degrades is the battery.
tags: [tech, phone, money]
---

Four-year cycle now. The camera and the speed have both been good enough
since about 2021, and the thing that actually gets worse is the battery.

A battery replacement is around 90 dollars and buys two more years. That
is the whole strategy.

Carrier trade-in deals are worth less than they look because they are
spread over 36 months of bill credits, which locks you in.

The one thing worth paying for is storage. It cannot be added later and
running out of it is what actually makes an old phone miserable.
