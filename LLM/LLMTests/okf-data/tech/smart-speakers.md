One in the kitchen, one in the main bedroom.

Actual daily use: kitchen timers while [baking](/kitchen/sourdough-loaf.md),
music, the weather, and turning off
[the bulbs](/tech/smart-bulbs.md) downstairs from upstairs.

Everything else that was demonstrated at purchase has gone unused: the
shopping list nobody checks, the calendar nobody trusts, the routines.

Microphone mute switch is on in the office during work hours. See
[work](/person/work.md).

They are on [the gadget network](/tech/iot-network.md).
