---
type: Note
title: Whether a VPN is worth it
description: Mostly not, for this household, and the reasons given in
  the ads are not the real ones.
tags: [tech, security, privacy, vpn]
---

Nearly everything is HTTPS now, so the coffee shop threat the ads describe
is largely historical.

A consumer VPN does not make you anonymous. It moves the trust from the
internet provider to the VPN company, which is a trade, not an
improvement, and the free ones sell the traffic.

Where it genuinely helps: hotel wifi that intercepts, and reaching
[the home server](/tech/home-server.md) from outside.

So: no commercial subscription. A self-hosted tunnel back to the house,
which covers both real cases and costs nothing.
