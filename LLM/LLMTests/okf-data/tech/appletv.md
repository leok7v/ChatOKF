Apple TV 4K, ethernet model, on the wired run behind
[the TV](/tech/smart-tv-livingroom.md).

The argument for it over the built-in apps or a cheaper stick is that it is
fast, there are no ads on the home screen, and the interface is consistent
enough that Ben can find what he wants without help.

All the [subscriptions](/watch/subscriptions.md) run through it. Family
sharing means one purchase works on everything.

The remote is small and disappears into the couch. There is a case with a
tag on it now.

Audio goes out over [HDMI ARC](/tech/hdmi-arc.md) to the soundbar.
