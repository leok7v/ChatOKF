Cable, 1000 down and 35 up, 80 dollars a month after the promotional rate
expired.

Download is not the constraint. Four simultaneous 4K streams is under 100
Mbps. The upload is what matters, and 35 is thin for a house with a person
on video calls all day and a
[cloud backup](/tech/nas-backup.md) running.

Fiber is available on the next street over and not on ours. Checked again
in 2025.

Own the modem rather than renting. The rental fee pays for a modem in
about eighteen months.
