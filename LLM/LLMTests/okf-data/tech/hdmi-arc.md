title: How HDMI ARC is wired here
description: One cable carries audio back from the TV to the soundbar,
  and it has to be in the port labeled ARC.
tags: [tech, audio, hdmi, wiring]
---

ARC means audio return channel: the same HDMI cable that carries video to
the TV carries audio back out to the soundbar. One cable, one remote for
volume.

It only works in the one HDMI port labeled ARC or eARC. Ours is HDMI 2 and
it is not the port the installer used.

CEC has to be on, and every manufacturer calls it something else. On this
set it is "Anynet". That is what lets the TV remote control
[the soundbar](/tech/soundbar.md) and what lets
[the Apple TV](/tech/appletv.md) turn the TV on.

When the audio drops out, the fix is almost always power cycling
everything in order: TV, then soundbar, then box.
