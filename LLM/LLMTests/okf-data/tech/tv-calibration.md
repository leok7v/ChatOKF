---
type: Note
title: Turning off everything the TV does by default
description: Motion smoothing, vivid mode and dynamic contrast all make
  the picture worse, and they are all on out of the box.
tags: [tech, tv, picture, settings]
---

Out of the box a TV is set to look bright on a showroom floor, not correct
in a living room.

Turn off:

- Motion smoothing, sometimes called motion interpolation. This is the
  soap opera effect. It invents frames between the real ones and makes
  film look like video.
- Vivid or dynamic picture mode. Use Filmmaker Mode or Cinema.
- Dynamic contrast and any "enhancer".
- Automatic content recognition, which reports what you watch.

Picture mode is set separately per input on most sets, which is why doing
it once does not stick. See
[the TV](/tech/smart-tv-livingroom.md).
