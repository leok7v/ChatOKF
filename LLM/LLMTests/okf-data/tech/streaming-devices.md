Living room: [Apple TV](/tech/appletv.md).

Bedroom: a Roku stick, three years old, still fine. Slower, more ads on
the home screen, and a different remote layout, which is why nobody but
Joe uses it without complaining.

Basement: the smart platform built into an older TV, which is genuinely
bad and is the reason the basement TV mostly goes unused.

Standardizing on one platform would be worth about forty dollars and a
Saturday. It has been on the list for two years.
