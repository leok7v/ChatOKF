description: A 3.1 soundbar with a wireless subwoofer, bought because
  the TV speakers could not carry dialogue.

---

3.1 channel with a wireless sub, under the TV.

The reason was dialogue. Flat-panel TVs fire their speakers down or back
and voices vanish under music. Everyone in the house was reaching for the
remote during every quiet scene.

Night mode, which compresses the dynamic range, is on permanently. It makes
loud things quieter and quiet things louder, which is exactly what a room
with sleeping kids needs.

Connected by [HDMI ARC](/tech/hdmi-arc.md), not optical, so the TV remote
controls the volume.
