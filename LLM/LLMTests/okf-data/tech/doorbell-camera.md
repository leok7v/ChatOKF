description: Useful for packages, and the subscription to keep
  recordings is the part nobody mentions at purchase.

---

Wired to the existing doorbell transformer, which needed upgrading from 10
VA to 16 VA before it would hold a charge.

Worth it for: package notifications and seeing who is at the door without
going downstairs.

Not worth it: the motion alerts as shipped, which fired on every car and
every squirrel until the zones were drawn tightly around the walk.

Recordings require a monthly subscription. Without it you get a live view
and a notification and nothing saved, which is not what most people think
they are buying.

On [the gadget network](/tech/iot-network.md).
