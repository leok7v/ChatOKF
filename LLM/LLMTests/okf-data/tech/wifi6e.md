Wi-Fi is a wireless local network following the IEEE 802.11 standards.
The generation numbers are marketing names for those standards.

- Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac): 5 GHz only for the fast part. Still fine.
- Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax): better in crowded conditions, not faster for one
  device. The real gain is many devices at once.
- Wi-Fi 6E: adds the 6 GHz band. More space, shorter range, does not
  penetrate walls. Excellent in one room, useless through a chimney.
- Wi-Fi 7: wider channels and using several bands at once.

For this house none of it is the bottleneck.
[The upload](/tech/isp-plan.md) is, and so is
[the chimney](/tech/wifi-dead-spot.md). A faster standard fixes neither.
