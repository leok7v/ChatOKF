One vault, shared with Dana, unlocked by a long passphrase nobody else
knows.

What it fixed: the same password on forty sites, and the fact that neither
of us could get into an account the other had set up.

Shared items: the [router admin](/tech/router-settings.md), the
[ISP account](/tech/isp-plan.md), the
[streaming subscriptions](/watch/subscriptions.md), the utilities, the
airline programs from [points and miles](/travel/points-and-miles.md).

Recovery is the hard part. The emergency kit is printed and in the fire
box, because a password manager you cannot get into is worse than no
password manager.

Two-factor on email first, before anything else.
