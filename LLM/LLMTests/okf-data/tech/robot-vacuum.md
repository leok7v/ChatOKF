title: The robot vacuum
description: Runs daily, handles the open floors, and cannot deal with
  cords or the dog's water bowl.
tags: [tech, smart-home, cleaning]
---

Runs at 10am on weekdays when the house is quiet.

It does not replace vacuuming. It keeps the open floor from needing it,
which is different and is still worth it with a 60-pound dog shedding.

It gets stuck on: phone charging cables, the bathroom scale, the fringe on
the dining room rug, and once the dog's water bowl, memorably.

Mapping and no-go zones fixed most of that. Emptying the bin is a chore
someone still has to do.

On [the gadget network](/tech/iot-network.md).
