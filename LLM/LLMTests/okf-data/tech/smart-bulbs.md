title: Smart bulbs
description: Six bulbs on a hub, useful for schedules and useless the
  moment a guest flips the wall switch.
tags: [tech, smart-home, lighting]
---

Six bulbs: living room lamps, the porch, the upstairs hall.

They work well for schedules. The porch light coming on at sunset without
anyone thinking about it is the single best thing in the smart home pile.

The flaw is structural: a smart bulb needs power at all times, so a wall
switch turned off makes it unreachable and unresponsive. Guests flip
switches. Smart switches rather than smart bulbs is the right answer and
would mean rewiring.

Hub on [the gadget network](/tech/iot-network.md), controlled from
[the speakers](/tech/smart-speakers.md).
