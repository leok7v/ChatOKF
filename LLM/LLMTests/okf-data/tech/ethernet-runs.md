Two runs of Cat6 from the basement panel: one up into the living room
behind the TV, one up two floors to the upstairs hall.

The living room run was easy, straight up through the floor into the wall
behind the TV.

The upstairs run went through a closet that happened to stack over the
basement stairs. Finding that path took longer than pulling the cable.

Keystone jacks at both ends, punched down, not crimped plugs. A cheap cable
tester before closing anything up.

This is what makes [the mesh](/tech/wifi-mesh.md) work and what feeds
[the TV](/tech/smart-tv-livingroom.md) without wifi.
