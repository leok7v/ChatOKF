A small low-power machine next to [the NAS](/tech/nas-backup.md), fifteen
watts idle, on a
[reserved address](/tech/router-settings.md).

Runs: the media library that serves to
[the Apple TV](/tech/appletv.md), a network-wide ad blocker, and the
tunnel that replaces [a commercial VPN](/tech/vpn.md).

The ad blocker is the one everyone in the house notices, and it is also
the one that breaks things occasionally in ways that are hard to diagnose
because nobody thinks of it.

Backed up like anything else. A server that holds the only copy of
something is a liability.
