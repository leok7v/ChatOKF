Measured with a laptop, wired reference first, spring 2025.

- Wired to the router: 940 down, 34 up.
- Living room, wired mesh node, 5 GHz: 610 down.
- Upstairs hall, wired node: 540 down.
- [Back bedroom](/tech/wifi-dead-spot.md): 380 down, -52 dBm. Was 45 down
  before [the mesh](/tech/wifi-mesh.md).
- Basement far corner: 210 down.

Every number is far past what any actual use needs. The point was the
dead spot, not the maximum.

Redo after any firmware change.
